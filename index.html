Quality Assurance

Pre-Press File Readiness Checklist

Follow these 4 simple prepress guidelines to guarantee accurate colors and zero bleed issues.

For best results, always export artwork as vector PDF files with all fonts converted to outlines/curves. We also accept Adobe Illustrator (.AI), Photoshop (.PSD), CorelDraw (.CDR), and uncompressed high-resolution TIFF/PNG images.

Digital presses process color using Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, and Key Black (CMYK). Designing in RGB may cause slight color shifts upon printing. Ensure your document setup color space is set to CMYK before exporting.

For crisp text and sharp graphic reproduction, ensure embedded images have an effective resolution of at least 300 DPI for standard documents and cards, and 150+ DPI for wide-format flex banners.

Include a 3mm (0.125 in) bleed margin on all cut edges where background colors or imagery extend to the border. Keep critical text and logos at least 4mm inside the safe margin line.
